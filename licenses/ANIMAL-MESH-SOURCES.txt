Waxholm Space atlas of the Sprague Dawley rat brain, v4.
Kleven H, Bjerke IE, Clasca F, Groenewegen HJ, Bjaalie JG, Leergaard TB (2023).
Waxholm Space atlas of the rat brain: a 3D atlas supporting data analysis and integration.
Nature Methods. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37783883/

Original MRI specimen: 80-day-old male Sprague Dawley rat, Duke Center for In Vivo Microscopy.
Acknowledgment: Neural Systems Laboratory, University of Oslo; Duke Center for In Vivo Microscopy.

Surface extraction/simplification: DANDI atlas project, root mask, 99,999 triangles.
Downloaded 2026-10-08 from:
https://atlas.dandiarchive.org/data/atlases/whs_sd/meshes/9999.glb?v=20260519
Pipeline: https://github.com/dandi/dandi-atlas/blob/main/scripts/build_rat_atlas.py
Local conversion: tools/prepare-animal-mesh.py.
Changes: converted coordinates from RAS to the application's presentation axes,
centered geometry, extracted positions and triangle indices. No further simplification.
Source SHA-256 and dimensions recorded in assets/rat-whs.json.
This surface is the whole atlas mask, not a segmented cortical pial surface.
Highlighted animal regions are separate schematic markers, not atlas segmentations.

Macaque, dog and cat: original schematic ellipsoid-based geometry generated in
comparative-lab.js. It does not derive from a transformed human mesh or MRI data.
Schematic folding is regular and does not represent anatomical sulci.
No CATLAS or macaque atlas data with unverified redistribution terms is bundled.

Human: existing FreeSurfer fsaverage pial / aseg surfaces. See FreeSurfer.txt.
